About Me

Background

I hold a BSc in Computer Engineering and an MSc in Computer Science with a focus on Reinforcement Learning. I am currently pursuing a PhD in Reinforcement Learning, deepening my expertise in how agents learn from interaction with their environments.

Research Focus

My research centers on reward signals and their impact over time in reinforcement learning environments. I study how the design and structure of rewards influence agent behavior, learning efficiency, and long-term performance across diverse domains.

Current Research

My latest work is DyLam, presented at AAMAS 2025.
